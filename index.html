About Me

Hey, I'm Jason Ntuuwa, a Software Development student at BYU-Idaho who loves turning ideas into things people can actually click, scroll, and interact with. I got hooked on web development through the WDD course sequence, where HTML, CSS, and JavaScript went from confusing symbols to actual tools I could build with. There's something satisfying about watching a blank page slowly turn into something real. When I'm not coding, you'll probably find me on the basketball court working on my jump shot, grinding through FIFA matches on Xbox (I take my squad very seriously), or falling down YouTube rabbit holes watching the latest inventions and tech breakthroughs. Mexican food is one of my go-tos too, especially street tacos with steak, lime, and cilantro, and when I'm really hungry, nothing hits quite like a torta. I guess you could say I like building things, watching other people build cool things, and eating really good tacos while I do it.
